- 제목: A Flamboyant Orion
- 설명: The Orion Nebula is one of the most representative nebulae observable in the winter. Its center is bright enough to view with the naked eye. The nebula usually resembles a radiant pink flower, but shows off quite a different image here through the use of narrowband imaging.
  In fact, the Orion Nebula is surrounded by interstellar clouds. It may not be obviously distinguishable in this picture, but shall we go ahead and try to look for it?
