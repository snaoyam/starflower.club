- 제목: Seagull’s Flight
- 설명: Seagull Nebula cannot be seen easily. With its brightness so dim, it is nearly impossible to film it in school. Furthermore, the nebula stays for a very short time low in the sky, making it even more difficult. Seagull hiding behind the darkness of the night sky blossomed in our hands to soar.
  ICT2177 refers to the head of the Seagull Nebula, and many astrophotographers combined the neighbouring interstellar clouds and nebula to call it the Seagull Nebula. The main viewing point of the picture is the seagull’s blue wings. This is a valuable sight only visible through narrow band imaging.
