- 제목: A Hook in the Sky
- 설명: A solar eclipse occurs when the Moon, located between the Earth and the Sun, blocks the Sun and creates a shadow. Before this curious phenomenon was explained, solar eclipses were deemed to be an ominous sign of what was to come. Now, it has become a global astronomical event enjoyed by the masses. A solar eclipse that covers the entire Sun, as shown in the image at left, is called a total solar eclipse.
